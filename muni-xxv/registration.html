Registration for MUNI XXV

MUNI XXV took place March 24-26, 2023, and registration for the conference is now closed.

For any registration, logistics, or general questions, please contact us at registration@illinoismun.org, logistics@illinoismun.org, or secretary@illinoismun.org.

  • Homewood Suites by Hilton Champaign-Urbana

Liability and Photo Release Forms

Delegates were asked to submit liability and photo release forms at registration in the hotel lobby, either in person or electronically to our USG of Registration at registration@illinoismun.org.
