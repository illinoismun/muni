Divij Garg
Secretary-General
LAS '26 | Computer Science & Mathematics
Hello! My name is Divij Garg, and I'm currently a senior in mathematics department, graduating from the College of LAS in May 2026! I have been a member of Illinois Model United Nations for my entire collegiate career, and have had the privilege of working with all the dedicated general body members that have made this conference possible.

This year I am honored to be serving as your Secretary-General for MUNI XXVIII.

In my years at MUNI, I have directed the United Nations Development Programme, United Nations Economic and Social Council for the Pacific, and co-directed our very first Ad-Hoc of the Secretary-General. Through each experience, I have learned the value of attention to detail, public speaking, and strong communication skills. Our organization has spent months curating exciting and challenging committees for our delegates, and we cannot wait for everyone to experience them.

Outside of MUN, I love building rockets, playing the violin, and doing landscape photography. If you have any questions or concerns regarding MUNI XXVIII, feel free to reach out at secgen@illinoismun.org! We can't wait to welcome everyone this April.
