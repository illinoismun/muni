European Commission

Type: General Assembly

About

The European Commission is the principal lawmaking body of the EU, with representatives from every member state. We will be modifying some of the structures of the Commission for use in MUNI. This committee will focus on discussing European strategic autonomy and European Competitiveness. European strategic autonomy is the idea that Europe needs to become a world power so it can defend its interests on the world stage on its own without needing the US. European competitiveness is a push to grow Europe’s economy after a decade and a half of stagnation, and to build European industry and companies that can compete with those in the US and China. Both topics deal with how Europe will respond to changing dynamics in the world, and whether it can come to a solution.
