League of Nations

Type: General Assembly

About

The year is 1926, six years after the Paris Peace conference, the set of treaties that officially ended the Great War and created the League of Nations, concluded. Europe is recovering, and it seems that the world may be entering a new era of peace and prosperity. Despite some early setbacks, the League of Nations is expanding and has begun to make major efforts to secure this new peaceful and prosperous world. The Assembly is the main representative body of the League of Nations, and it has broad power to deliberate “any matter within the sphere of action of the League affecting the peace of the world”. In this committee delegates will deliberate and hope to ratify treaties to solve the great issues of our time, from the disarmament of the world to the illicit trade in narcotics.
