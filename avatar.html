Yu Dao

Type: Crisis Committee

About

“Water, Earth, Fire, Air. Long ago, the four nations lived in harmony. Then, everything changed when the Fire Nation attacked. Only the Avatar, Master of all four elements, could stop them, but when the world needed him most, he vanished.” (Katara, Avatar: The Last Airbender)

A hundred years passed before Katara, the last Southern Waterbender, freed the young Avatar Aang from an iceberg. Together with Katara, her brother Sokka, Toph Beifong, Suki of the Kyoshi Warriors, and eventually his former enemy, the exiled Fire Nation prince Zuko, Aang mastered the four elements and defeated Fire Lord Ozai, bringing the Hundred-Year War to an end. Zuko ascended to the throne and pledged to lead the Fire Nation toward peace, repentance, and cooperation with the other nations.

Yet barely a year after the war's conclusion, the fragile peace faces a new challenge. The Fire Nation has abruptly withdrawn from the Harmony Restoration Movement (HRM), which seeks to dismantle Fire Nation colonies on Earth Kingdom territory. The withdrawal comes as the movement turns its attention to the oldest colonies, including Yu Dao, a thriving metropolis whose inhabitants have built their lives across the physical and cultural boundaries of two nations for generations. Tensions are mounting, and the prospect of renewed conflict looms over a world still recovering from a century of war.

You, the foremost representatives of the four nations and Yu Dao, have now gathered for an emergency conference at Avatar Aang’s call. Can you reach an agreement and reconcile the demands of historical justice with the rights and aspirations of those who call the colonies home? The future of several communities and a world weary of war hangs in the balance.
