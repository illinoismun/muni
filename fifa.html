FIFA Executive Committee

Type: General Assembly

About

The FIFA Executive Committee (ExCo) is the First Committee of this Model United Nations General Assembly. Its main focus is on the governance of FIFA, which is the governing body of world football, which includes its tournaments, especially the World Cup, the biggest global sports tournament on Earth, and the distribution of FIFA's money. This year, ExCo's focusing on a July 2026 proposal, by FIFA, to open the World Cup up to private investors, and allowing them to hold stakes in the tournament, under the justification that it will generate money for football development, worldwide. However, said proposal has resulted in an immediate and furious backlash, among the football, and overall sports world, as it's viewed as literally selling out the World Cup, all without FIFA's member nations even being informed about this, at all. Delegates will be required to think about if this initiative will benefit their nation/confederation, if there is any alternative plan to generate football development, should reforms be undertaken at FIFA, to prevent this from happening/ever happening again, should the FIFA President step down, should they boycott FIFA competitions/start their own, should someone challenge the FIFA President, among many others!
