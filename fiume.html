City State of Fiume

Type: Crisis Committee

About

“Whose City Is It Anyway?” is a historical crisis committee set in Fiume, now Rijeka, in November 1920. Following WWI, the city became a disputed territory occupied and governed by Italian poet and nationalist Gabriele D’Annunzio. Delegates will represent real political leaders, military figures, artists, and labor activists with competing goals, including, but not limited to, annexation, independence, and social revolution. Beginning just after the Treaty of Rapallo declared Fiume an independent state, the committee will address the city’s uncertain political future, hallmarked by the possibility of outside military intervention.
