All-American MUN x MUNI XXVIII

We are proud to announce that All-American Model United Nations is a proud sponsor of MUNI XXVIII! Their continued support empowers our conference to provide delegates with world-class academic experiences, innovative committee sessions, and leadership opportunities. With this partnership, All-American MUN will provide free training resources for all MUNI delegates, allowing them to practice before the conference.

All-American MUN is a nationwide initiative that connects and elevates student leaders through Model UN training, coaching, and international programs. Their mission—to help students grow as speakers, writers, and global citizens—aligns perfectly with MUNI’s dedication to fostering diplomacy and collaboration.
